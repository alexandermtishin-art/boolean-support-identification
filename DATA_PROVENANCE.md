# Data provenance

## Experimental datasets

- Shis et al., *ACS Synthetic Biology* (2014), DOI 10.1021/sb500262f.
- Gorochowski et al., *Molecular Systems Biology* (2017), DOI 10.15252/msb.20167461.
- Weinberg et al., *Nature Biotechnology* (2017), DOI 10.1038/nbt.3805.

Files in the `experimental/` directory of the ZIP are **derived analysis results**, not original raw laboratory measurements. Obtain original supplementary measurements from their original publishers, subject to their reuse terms.

## Boolean networks

Historical scripts use published Boolean-network / CANA-derived materials. The original corpus is not included here pending redistribution rights verification.

## Status

The archive aggregates research and audit files dated October 21–22, 2026. Packaging is not equivalent to independently regenerating every historical numerical result. The current numerical five-variable optimum is unresolved.
